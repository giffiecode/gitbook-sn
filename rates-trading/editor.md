# Long Rate

**Long Rate = Pay Fixed Rate, Earn Floating Rate**&#x20;

_(Directly benefits from rising implied rates. Indirectly benefits from rising floating rate.)_



Entering a Long Rate position means **paying the fixed rate** and **earning the floating rate** from the underlying market. This position benefits when floating rates rise above the fixed rate. A Long Rate position is typically used to **hedge floating-rate liabilities**—for example, when a user has a variable-rate loan on Aave or Morpho and wants to lock in a predictable borrowing cost.



In practice, taking a Long Rate converts a floating-rate exposure into a **synthetic fixed-rate position**, stabilizing funding costs over the selected tenor.&#x20;
