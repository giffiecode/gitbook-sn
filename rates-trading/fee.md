# Fee

Supernova Interest Rate Market revenue from two sources:<br>

1. **Trading Fee (5 bps)**\
   A 0.05% fee is charged on the notional value of each rate trade and deducted from margin when opening a position.

For example, opening a six-month $250,000 position costs $125. Opening and later closing the same position would incur a total of $250 in trading fees. The position becomes profitable only if the implied rate moves by more than 10 bps in the trader’s favor.



2. **Settlement Fee (15 bps)**\
   A 0.15% fee is applied to the fixed-rate side of each position during settlement.

If the market’s fixed rate is 6%, long positions effectively pay 6.15%, while short positions receive 5.85%.&#x20;



During the initial phase, all fee is directed to the vault to reward liquidity providers.\
